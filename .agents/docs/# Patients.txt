Problem statement
Patient safety is heavily time constrainted, session times and medication times are usually Loosely directed by doctors and there is a huge miscommunication problems that directly effect patient vitals
such as:
Mismatching medication prescription or doctors forgotting to update prescription due to the huge number of managed patients
Or nurses simply missing medication date or vital patient notes due to paper work overhead which directly translates to Machine data loss and computer vision by nurse Or/And patient
Doctors want a simple and SECURE Way to reach nurses or patients, Docors spend 
and doctors must possess a reliable way to verify and update prescribed medication based on an emperical data driven approach, 
CRITICAL: A critical risk is (for exmple) a certain collective mineral decline in patients indicates common equippment failure, these types of patterns almost always go unnoticable
which can mitigate risks like:
- Patient Suffocation or insant Anemia (i made that term up)
- Collective poisoning
- Quality assurance


Patient Information to be Digitized
Per-Session Records (collected by nurses at every dialysis session)

Session identification

Which patient, which session, the date, and which machine and nurse handled it — so that any issue can later be traced back to a specific machine or shift if patterns emerge

Before the session starts

Patient's weight on arrival
The doctor-set "target" (dry) weight for that patient, and how much fluid has built up since the last session
Blood pressure and pulse before starting
Temperature

During the session

The fluid removal rate planned by the doctor versus what actually happened
The dialysis machine's settings (salt, potassium, calcium, and bicarbonate levels in the fluid), since these should be based on the patient's history and labs rather than left generic
Blood and dialysate flow rates
Blood-thinner dosage
How long the session was supposed to last versus how long it actually took
Blood pressure checks taken during the session, not just before and after, since drops in pressure often happen mid-treatment
Any incident that occurs — fainting, cramping, access problems, machine alarms — recorded in a way that can later be searched and counted, not just written as a note

After the session

Patient's weight on leaving
Blood pressure and pulse after finishing
How much fluid was actually removed
Whether any complications occurred

Medication given during the session

What was given, what dose, and at what time
Who administered it
The gap between when it was supposed to be given and when it was actually given — this is the exact miscommunication problem the project is meant to solve
Monthly Blood Test Records (ordered by doctors)
Hemoglobin and hematocrit (used to judge anemia and oxygen needs during sessions)
Iron levels (ferritin) and iron saturation, tracked together over months, exactly as the current manual practice already tries to do — but with automation instead of memory and paperwork
Potassium, sodium, calcium, and phosphorus levels
Parathyroid hormone level
Albumin (nutrition indicator)
Urea levels before and after dialysis, used to calculate how effective each session was
A general dialysis-adequacy score
Creatinine
Bicarbonate level
Inflammation marker, to help catch problems before they become visible symptoms



Suggested solutions:
1. Patient safety awareness system (highest priority)
- Unreliable following of Medical Constraints
- Dialysis Patient vitals (Dry weight trend analysis, BP Analysis, ) and track any arising or potential health issue regarding the patients
2. Patient Data collection and automation (eliminating paper work)
- Doctor diagnosis data collection and preprocessing
- Automatic dalysis prescription system using AI, ML, LLMs and Data analysis to analyze the trends of each patient to Automatically notify the nurse with the details of each medication and notices 
- All nurses know all the risks and dangers about any patient at any time due to the instant connecion with the doctors office 
- Nurses interface (Desktop on counter or/And application)
- (optional) Patient interface
3. Medical diagnosis trend analysis and intelligent insights system
- Medical analysis and pattern recognition that takes the patient data and outputs a prescription and assessments reliably and algorithmically
4. MedGemma integration, an added layer of security and assistance for patients 
5. An IoT integrated alert system for nurses desktop and mobile dashboards and doctor consensus system for critical decisions
6. Anonymous Reporting mechanism for patients
